Gas logs and inserts

Want the look of a fire without loading wood, or need to replace an old gas log set?

A gas log set sits in an existing fireplace; an insert is a complete unit that fits into the opening. Which one suits yours depends on the fireplace, its condition, the gas supply and the venting each product requires. Measure the opening and send photos of the fireplace and its model plate, and we can compare the log sets and inserts that fit.

Questions people ask

Do vent-free logs need a chimney?

They do not use a chimney to vent combustion gases outdoors. That does not make them suitable for every fireplace or room. The appliance’s instructions and local requirements must be checked before choosing them.

What is the difference between gas logs and an insert?

A gas log set places a burner and logs in a compatible fireplace. An insert is an appliance fitted into a suitable existing opening, with its own venting requirements. They are not interchangeable.

Can you convert my wood fireplace to gas?

Possibly, depending on its construction, condition and the available gas supply. Photos, measurements and the model plate help establish which options to assess.
